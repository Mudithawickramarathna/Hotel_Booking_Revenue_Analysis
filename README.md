# Hotel Booking & Revenue Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes hotel booking and revenue data using Microsoft Power BI to identify patterns in hotel performance, room demand, pricing, customer segments and revenue trends.

The project transforms raw hotel management data into an interactive three-page dashboard designed to support data-driven business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall hotel revenue and booking performance
- Compare revenue performance across hotels
- Identify high-performing room types
- Analyze room demand and room bookings
- Examine customer types and booking patterns
- Identify important revenue trends over time
- Generate business insights and recommendations

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains **10,000 hotel booking records** and includes information about:

- Hotel Name
- Room Type
- City
- Country
- Payment Mode
- Price
- Room Available
- Room Booked
- Booking Date
- Customer ID
- Customer Type
- Room Type Count
- Total Revenue
- Discount
- Customer Name
- Customer Age
- Customer Gender

> **Note:** The source dataset does not specify a currency for monetary fields. Therefore, monetary values are presented in their original numerical units without assigning a specific currency.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

1. **Hotel Paradise generated the highest revenue** among the hotels analyzed, identifying it as the strongest revenue contributor in the dataset.

2. **Business Class was the highest revenue-generating room type**, indicating its importance to overall revenue performance.

3. **January 2022 recorded the highest monthly revenue**, making it an important period for understanding peak revenue performance.

4. **Existing customers represented the leading customer type**, while Kazakhstan was the leading booking country.

---

## 📋 Business Recommendations

1. Identify and evaluate the practices associated with the strong performance of Hotel Paradise and consider applying relevant strategies to other hotels.

2. Maintain focus on Business Class while evaluating opportunities to improve the performance of other room categories.

3. Investigate the factors associated with the strong January 2022 revenue performance to support future revenue planning.
